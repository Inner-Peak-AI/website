How it works

Five minutes a day, twice a week, in advisory.

  1. Students set goals

    Better sleep, less stress, more confidence, getting along with friends.

  2. They check in each day

    A quick mood check-in and a short trail activity, often during advisory.

  3. They build skills

    Personalized videos, practice exercises and feedback, all created by licensed therapists.

  4. They get support when they need it

    24/7, judgment-free journaling and guidance, with a clear path to a real adult when it matters.

  5. You see the big picture

    Dashboards show trends by grade and building, and high-risk students are flagged to your team immediately.

Is this an AI companion?

No. And that's on purpose.

Kids don't need AI friends; they need real ones. Inner Peak uses AI only to personalize and deliver content created by real therapists. It's designed to point kids back toward the people in their lives (parents, friends, teachers and counselors), not to replace them.

Inner Peak never gives medical advice and is not a substitute for therapy.

Does Inner Peak replace therapy?

No. It builds everyday coping skills and helps kids recognize when to reach out to a trusted adult or professional.
